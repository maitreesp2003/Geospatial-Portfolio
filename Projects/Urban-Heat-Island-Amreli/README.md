# 🖼️ Project Outputs

## 1. 📍 Study Area Map

This map illustrates the geographical location and administrative boundaries of **Amreli District, Gujarat**, selected as the study area for the Urban Heat Island (UHI) assessment. It provides the geographical context and spatial extent of the study used for all subsequent geospatial analyses.

---

## 2. 🔄 Methodology Flowchart

This flowchart presents the complete methodology adopted for the study, including satellite data acquisition, image preprocessing, calculation of geospatial indices, Land Surface Temperature retrieval, Urban Heat Island mapping, statistical analysis, and interpretation of results.

---

## 3. 🌿 Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI)

These maps illustrate the spatial distribution of vegetation across Amreli District for **2000** and **2025**. NDVI was used to evaluate changes in vegetation cover and to examine its relationship with land surface temperature and Urban Heat Island intensity.

---

## 4. 🏙️ Normalized Difference Built-up Index (NDBI)

These maps represent the spatial distribution of built-up areas in **2000** and **2025**, highlighting urban expansion and changes in impervious surfaces over the study period.

---

## 5. 🗺️ Land Use / Land Cover (LULC)

These classified maps illustrate the land use and land cover patterns of Amreli District for **2000** and **2025**. The comparison provides insights into changes in vegetation, agricultural land, built-up areas, water bodies, and other land cover classes over time.

---

## 6. 🌡️ Land Surface Temperature (LST)

These maps present the spatial variation in land surface temperature for **2000** and **2025**, identifying areas with different thermal characteristics and highlighting regions experiencing higher surface temperatures.

---

## 7. 🔥 Urban Heat Island (UHI)

These maps depict the spatial distribution of Urban Heat Island intensity across Amreli District for **2000** and **2025**. They illustrate the development and expansion of thermal hotspots associated with urbanization and land use changes.

---

## 8. 📊 Correlation Analysis (2000)

This analysis examines the relationship between vegetation (NDVI), built-up areas (NDBI), and land surface temperature for the year **2000**, providing insights into the factors influencing Urban Heat Island formation.

---

## 9. 📊 Correlation Analysis (2025)

This analysis evaluates the relationship between vegetation, built-up areas, and land surface temperature for the year **2025**, highlighting how these relationships have evolved over the study period.

---

## 10. 📈 Mann–Kendall Trend Analysis (2000–2025)

This figure presents the long-term trend analysis of Urban Heat Island intensity using the **Mann–Kendall statistical test**, allowing the identification of significant increasing or decreasing temperature trends over the 26-year study period.

---

## 11. 📉 Annual Correlation Matrix (2000–2025)

This matrix summarizes the annual correlations among **NDVI, NDBI, LST, and UHI**, providing a comprehensive overview of the interactions between vegetation, urbanization, and thermal characteristics throughout the study period.

---

## 12. 🌿 Temporal Trends of NDVI (2000–2025)

This graph illustrates the temporal variation in vegetation conditions from **2000 to 2025**, enabling the assessment of long-term changes in vegetation cover across the study area.

---

## 13. 🏙️ Temporal Trends of NDBI (2000–2025)

This graph presents the long-term variation in built-up area intensity between **2000 and 2025**, reflecting patterns of urban growth and land development.

---

## 14. 🌡️ Temporal Trends of LST (2000–2025)

This graph illustrates the temporal variation in land surface temperature throughout the study period, highlighting long-term thermal changes across Amreli District.

---

## 15. 🔥 Temporal Trends of UHI (2000–2025)

This graph presents the long-term variation in Urban Heat Island intensity from **2000 to 2025**, providing insights into the evolution of urban thermal environments and the impacts of land use changes over time.
