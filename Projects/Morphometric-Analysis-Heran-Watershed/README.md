# 🖼️ Project Outputs

## 1. 🔄 Methodology Flowchart

This flowchart illustrates the complete workflow adopted for the GIS-based morphometric analysis of the Heran Watershed. It outlines the sequence of data acquisition, DEM processing, drainage extraction, watershed delineation, and the computation of linear, areal, and relief morphometric parameters.

---

## 2. 📍 Study Area Map

This map presents the geographical location and spatial extent of the Heran Watershed in Gujarat. It provides the geographical context of the study area and serves as the reference for all subsequent spatial analyses performed in this project.

---

## 3. 🏞️ Geomorphology of the Basin Area

This map illustrates the major geomorphological features of the watershed, highlighting different landforms and terrain characteristics. Understanding these features helps interpret the physical landscape and its influence on drainage development.

---

## 4. 🪨 Geology Map of the Basin Area

This map represents the geological formations present within the watershed. Geological characteristics influence drainage patterns, soil formation, infiltration, erosion processes, and overall watershed behavior.

---

## 5. 🌿 Land Use/Land Cover (LULC) Map

This map depicts the distribution of different land use and land cover classes across the watershed, including vegetation, agricultural land, settlements, and water bodies. The analysis provides valuable information on surface characteristics affecting watershed dynamics.

---

## 6. 🌊 Drainage Network Map

This map shows the extracted drainage network derived from the Digital Elevation Model (DEM). It illustrates the hierarchy of stream channels and forms the foundation for calculating the morphometric parameters used in this study.

---

## 7. 📈 Stream Length vs. Stream Order

This graph illustrates the relationship between stream order and stream length within the watershed. It provides insights into the drainage characteristics and supports the interpretation of watershed morphology and hydrological behavior.

---

## 8. 💧 Drainage Density Map

This map displays the spatial variation in drainage density throughout the watershed. Drainage density is an important morphometric parameter that reflects runoff characteristics, infiltration capacity, and landscape dissection.

---

## 9. 📋 Linear Morphometric Parameters

This table summarizes the calculated linear morphometric parameters, including stream order, stream number, stream length, bifurcation ratio, and related indices used to characterize the drainage network.

---

## 10. 📋 Areal Morphometric Parameters

This table presents the computed areal morphometric parameters, such as drainage density, stream frequency, form factor, elongation ratio, circularity ratio, and other indices describing the watershed geometry.

---

## 11. 📋 Relief Morphometric Parameters

This table summarizes the relief morphometric parameters, including basin relief, relief ratio, ruggedness number, and other topographic characteristics that describe the elevation and terrain variability of the watershed.
